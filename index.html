Owewise

A simple digital diary for money you lend and owe.

About the app

Owewise helps you keep a clear record of who owes you and whom you owe. Add entries in seconds, track what has been paid, and never lose count of an udhar again.

Features

  • Record money given and taken with name, amount, date and notes
  • Track pending and settled entries
  • Your data is saved to your account so it is not lost with your phone
  • Free to use with ads, or go ad-free with a low-cost premium subscription
