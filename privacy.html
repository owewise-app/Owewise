Privacy Policy

App: Owewise  |  Developer: TechCoder  |  Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TechCoder ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the Owewise mobile app ("the App"). Owewise helps you record and track money you lend or owe (a digital udhar diary). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide:

  • Account information: such as your email address or phone number and a user ID, if you create an account or sign in.
  • Ledger entries: the details you type into the App, such as names of people, amounts, dates, notes and payment status.

Information collected automatically:

  • Device and usage information: such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, crash logs and general diagnostic data.
  • Advertising identifiers: the Android Advertising ID and similar identifiers, collected by our advertising partner (see Section 4).
  • Purchase information: subscription status and transaction identifiers. We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details. Payments are processed by Google Play.

We do not collect your precise location, and we do not access your contacts, photos, microphone or camera unless a feature clearly asks for your permission first.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, maintain and sync your ledger data in the App.
  • To create and manage your account and keep it secure.
  • To manage subscriptions and unlock premium features.
  • To show advertisements in the free version of the App.
  • To fix bugs, improve performance and develop new features.
  • To respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

3. Where your data is stored

Your account and ledger data are stored on servers provided by Supabase (supabase.com/privacy), our database and authentication provider. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Supabase may process data in data centers located outside your country.

4. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): may use the advertising ID and device information to show ads. How Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • Google Play Billing / Google Play Services (payments): Google Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat (subscription management): receives an app user ID and purchase information to verify and manage subscriptions. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase (database and authentication): Supabase Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising choices

The free version of the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. Subscribing to the premium plan removes ads.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • with the service providers listed above, who process data on our behalf to run the App;
  • when required by law, court order or a valid legal request;
  • to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the App or the public;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case you will be notified of any change in ownership.

7. Information about other people

Owewise lets you store names and amounts relating to other people (such as friends, family or customers). You are responsible for entering such information lawfully and for using it only for your own personal record keeping. We do not contact these people or use their details for any other purpose.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing owewise.help@zohomail.in from the email address linked to your account, or by using the account deletion option in the App where available. We will delete your data within 30 days of a verified request, except where we must keep certain records to comply with the law. Data held by Google Play (such as purchase records) is governed by Google's policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, download or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you may exercise these rights by contacting us at the email below.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your login details safe.

11. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
